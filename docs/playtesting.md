Watch a first attempt without coaching; then allow the on-page driving hint.
Ask the player to describe what the turn arrow, target and five splits mean.
Try a missed bend, a hillside excursion, pause/focus loss and retry as well as
an ideal lap. Note whether the car visibly follows the ground after recovery.

Recruit 3–5 people if available, including someone new to rally games. A useful
initial acceptance target is that a majority can beat Bracken in Finch 1300 within
three attempts, understand how to unlock the next track, and recover from one
mistake without abandoning the session. Report the actual counts and attempts;
this small formative sample is not a population estimate. Do not silently loosen
