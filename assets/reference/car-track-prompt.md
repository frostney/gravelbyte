## Reference review

The image clearly distinguishes the three car silhouettes, difficulty profiles,
stat labels and seasonal palettes. Its scenery and typography are more detailed
than a literal 120x120 framebuffer; use it for art direction and selection layout,
not as a device screenshot or a new performance/fidelity requirement.

## Accepted implementation refinements

The user approved this visual direction, then requested a more minimal car
selector: remove all selection counts and numeric stat fractions. Use the
car name, difficulty and unnumbered speed/acceleration/drift bars. The new
courses are Sunmeadow Run and Frostpine Pass. In-game hints follow PicoSystem,
keyboard, gamepad or touch controls. The artwork remains a reference, not UI assets.
