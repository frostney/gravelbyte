TAKE IT OFFLINE

Made for PicoSystem.

The same game, on the small screen it was built for.

  1. Connect your PicoSystem by USB.
  2. Hold X while switching it on. A drive named RPI-RP2 appears.
  3. Copy gravelbyte.uf2 to that drive. The device restarts into the game.

PicoSystem controls: D-pad steers and selects · A gas / confirm · B brake / back · X drift · Y pause. X toggles sound on the title/pause screen and records after finishing. Use A to retry or B to return to track selection.
